The math behind the move

Price versus probability

A call is profitable when your chance of winning is at least the break-even equity demanded by the pot.

Required equity call ÷ (pot + bet + call)
One card to come outs ÷ 46
Two cards to come 1 − miss turn × miss river

Decision rule: call when draw equity ≥ required equity; otherwise fold. These drills treat the listed clean outs as your winning paths so you can focus on the calculation.
