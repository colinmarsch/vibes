The one formula

Call ÷ final pot

Add the starting pot, your opponent's bet, and your call. Then divide the amount you call by that final pot.

Required equity call ÷ (pot + bet + call)

At the table: if the pot is $100 and the bet is $50, you call $50 to play for a final pot of $200. That means you need 25%.
